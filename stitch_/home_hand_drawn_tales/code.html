The Beauty of Empty Space

In our studio, we honor Ma—the intentional void between notes, the silence between words, and the white space on the canvas.

We believe that a story isn't just told by what we draw, but by what we leave for the audience to imagine. This philosophy of "slow-living" animation allows every frame to breathe, creating an immersive experience that feels as lived-in as an old house.
